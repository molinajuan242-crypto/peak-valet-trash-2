Our Services

🚪 Doorstep Valet Trash

Our primary service. Residents place properly tied trash bags outside their doors, and our team collects them on the property's scheduled service days.

🏢 Apartment Community Service

Designed specifically for multifamily properties, with service organized around your property's buildings, units, and needs.

🌙 Scheduled Collection

Reliable pickup schedules that work with your property's operations and resident expectations.

🧹 Apartment Turnover Cleaning

An optional add-on for vacant apartments after a resident moves out. This service helps prepare a unit for the next resident and is not regular housekeeping or recurring residential cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What days do you provide service?

Pickup schedules are established around each property's needs, operations, and resident expectations.

What types of trash do you collect?

We collect properly tied household trash bags and other items permitted by the property's trash and disposal rules.

Can you work with an existing property management company?

Yes. Peak is designed to work directly with apartment communities and property management teams.

Do you provide turnover cleaning?

Yes. Optional turnover cleaning is available for vacant apartments after a resident moves out and is quoted separately.

How does a community get started?

Submit a property quote request with your community details so we can discuss your service needs.

Can service be customized?

Yes. Service plans can be structured around unit count, buildings, layout, pickup frequency, and community requirements.
