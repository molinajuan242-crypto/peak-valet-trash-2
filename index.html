How Valet Trash Works

Residents Place Trash Outside

2

3

What We Collect

✓ Properly tied household trash bags
✓ Everyday residential household waste
✓ Items permitted by your property's trash and disposal rules

Oversized items, loose trash, hazardous materials, and prohibited items are not part of standard valet trash service. Final collection rules can be established with the property.
